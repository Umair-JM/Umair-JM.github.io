01 / About

About me

I'm a researcher and educator with a computer-engineering foundation and a deep interest in cybersecurity. My research career began in the COGNET Lab at NUST, where I worked on quantum-resistant cryptography, defending deep-learning models against adversarial attacks, and verifiable, tamper-evident voting systems combining computer vision and cryptography.

Alongside research I've spent years teaching — designing and delivering technical courses, supervising student projects, and being recognised with a Best Instructor award for an average of 9.15/10 across six performance metrics. Today I'm pursuing a PhD at AUT at the intersection of health and artificial intelligence, developing an AI-based biomarker to identify chronic lower-back pain.

I hold an extensive stack of cybersecurity certifications across IBM, Cisco and TryHackMe — spanning penetration testing, threat intelligence, incident response and forensics, network defense and endpoint security.
